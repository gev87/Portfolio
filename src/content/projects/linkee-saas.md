## A contract that kept going

Linkee.ai is an AI SaaS product I have been building frontend for since
October 2021, part-time and remote, alongside my full-time roles. It is the
longest-running engagement on this page, and the one where I have watched
decisions age.

## Product surface

I build dynamic pages and reusable React components that extend the core
product — the work of turning a feature that exists in principle into one a
user can reach.

## Making behaviour visible

I instrumented Mixpanel event tracking across the product. The point was not
the dashboard; it was that questions about how people actually used a feature
stopped being answered by whoever argued hardest, and started being answered
by data.

## Stability

I also spent a meaningful share of the contract on stability: resolving
